About us

An infrastructure service providing company with extensive international and national experience, offering fully integrated supply and construction services on a turnkey basis.

FABTECH Bangladesh

Fully integrated supply & construction

FabTech Bangladesh is an infrastructure service providing company with extensive international and national experience, offering fully integrated supply and construction services to the power plant, oil and gas industry (refinery, LPG plant, petrochemical, pipeline), fertilizer, chemical, cement factory and pharmaceutical industries — in the mechanical, electrical with instrumentation and civil sectors, on a turnkey basis.

We have seasoned experience of civil and electro-mechanical works, including fabrication, installation and commissioning of power plants, heavy and light manufacturing facilities and oil and gas industry installations — refineries, LPG plants, petrochemical facilities and pipelines.

FabTech Bangladesh is committed to provide quality services safely, within budget and within the prescribed time frame.
