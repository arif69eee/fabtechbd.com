Quality & HSE

Quality control, quality assurance and safety are managed in-house — not subcontracted — so standards hold from the workshop through to hand-over.

Policy

Built in, not bolted on

Quality policy

  • FABTECH offers any kind of construction work conforming to the approved code and conduct.
  • FABTECH basically follows ASTM, BNBC, BS and IS.
  • To ensure field quality control, quality assurance and quality management, FABTECH has established an in-house QA/QC team along with laboratory facilities.

Health, Safety & Environment

  • FABTECH has its own health, environment and safety policy for construction work.
  • FABTECH has established a dedicated in-house HSE team to ensure maximum safety for workers.
  • FABTECH generally follows codes from the Occupational Safety & Health Administration (OSHA) — although the client’s requirement is the first thing FABTECH follows.
In practice

How it shows up on site

Weld & material control

Procedure-controlled welding with certified welders, traceable materials and documented inspection to project code.

Testing before hand-over

Hydrostatic and load testing of process piping, pipelines, storage tanks and pressure vessels prior to commissioning.

Client rules first

Where a client’s HSE requirement is stricter than our own baseline, the client’s requirement governs.
